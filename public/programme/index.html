Programme

Times are local. All events are free unless noted. The programme may change with weather; check this page on the day.

  1. Fri 8 Oct, 19:00

    Opening Lantern Parade

    Starlight PierHandmade lanterns lead the crowd along the waterfront.

  2. Nightly 8–17 Oct, 20:00 and 21:00

    Harbour Light Show

    Lantern PlazaA 12-minute show of light and music across the water.

  3. Sat 9 Oct, 14:00

    Story Boats Family Afternoon

    Old Dock HallStorytelling and paper-boat workshops for ages 4 and up.

  4. Sat–Sun 9–10 and 16–17 Oct, 16:00–22:00

    Makers' Night Market

    Market StepsLocal craft, snacks and design. Bring a bag; stalls are encouraged to avoid single-use plastic.

  5. Sun 17 Oct, 20:00

    Closing Concert Under the Lights

    Starlight PierLive music from local bands, followed by a final lantern release of reusable lanterns.
