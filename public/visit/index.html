Plan your visit

Entry is free and no booking is needed, except for the family workshops.

Common questions

Do I need a ticket?

No. Events are free to attend. Story Boats workshops have limited places; arrive 30 minutes early to register.

Is the festival accessible?

The main promenade, Starlight Pier and Lantern Plaza are step-free. Wheelchair-accessible toilets are at each venue. Sessions with sign-language interpretation and relaxed sessions with lower sound are marked in the programme. Assistance dogs are welcome.

What if it rains?

Most events go ahead in light rain. In a rainstorm warning the light show and outdoor events pause; updates are posted on this website.

Can I bring children?

Yes. Under-12s should stay with an adult. A baby-care room is open at Old Dock Hall.
