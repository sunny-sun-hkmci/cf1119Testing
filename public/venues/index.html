Venues and travel

All venues are within a ten-minute walk of each other along the waterfront.

Starlight Pier

Opening parade and closing concert. Large open deck with step-free access.

Lantern Plaza

The nightly light show. Benches and shaded seating along the east side.

Market Steps

Makers' night market. Wide level path; the steps themselves have a ramp beside them.

Old Dock Hall

Family workshops. Indoor, air-conditioned, with a quiet room.

Getting there

We encourage public transport. Festival shuttle buses run every 10 minutes from 17:00 to 23:30 between the main station and Starlight Pier. There is no public car parking at the waterfront during the festival.

Cycling is welcome: free bicycle parking is at the west end of Lantern Plaza.
